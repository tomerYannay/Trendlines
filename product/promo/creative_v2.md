# Diago Promo v2 — "The Loss We Published"

45 seconds · 1280x720 · continuous pacing (gaps ≤0.4s) · CSS/SVG film engine · ElevenLabs v3 narration

---

## 1. Concept

Cold open on the one thing no trading service ever shows: a loss, published on purpose. PARA −94%, red, alone on screen — before the viewer even knows what Diago is. This inverts the genre: every competitor opens with a win, so opening with a loss is the fastest possible proof that "radical transparency" isn't a slogan. Only after trust is earned do we show the machine (2,259 stocks, nightly), the product moment (one alert, one score), and the strongest setup (the support bounce). The CTA closes the loop: "Every signal public. Even the losses." Diago is the character throughout — the marketing describes what Diago DOES, never what it promises.

## 3. Narration script — ElevenLabs blocks (paste one block per scene)

Voice: same as v1 (calm, low, confident male). No SSML — punctuation carries the delivery.

**Block 1 (S1, 0.0–6.0)**
Delivery: slow, flat, almost confessional. A beat after "losses." Land hard on "purpose."
```
This is one of our losses. We published it. On purpose.
```

**Block 2 (S2, 6.0–12.0)**
Delivery: quieter still. The ellipsis is a real pause — let it breathe. "Everything" is the key word of the whole film.
```
Because the wins mean nothing… unless you see everything.
```

**Block 3 (S3, 12.0–22.0)**
Delivery: tempo lifts — this is the engine-room reveal. Steady build through the number, slight punch on "catches."
```
Every night, Diago redraws the trendlines on two thousand, two hundred and fifty-nine stocks. When a price breaks its diagonal — Diago catches it.
```

**Block 4 (S4, 22.0–31.0)**
Delivery: crisp, matter-of-fact. Short clauses, clean stops. "Rare" gets a small lift, not hype.
```
You get one alert. One score — built from seventy-two thousand historical setups. Six means decent. Ten means rare.
```

**Block 5 (S5, 31.0–38.0)**
Delivery: rhythmic — mirror the price action: falls… holds… bounces. Warm on the last sentence; this is the favorite child.
```
It works on support too. Price falls to its rising line, holds — and bounces. Diago's strongest setup.
```

**Block 6 (S6, 38.0–45.0)**
Delivery: settled, unhurried close. Even pacing through the offer. Final line slower — "Even the losses" is the callback to Block 1; let it land and stop.
```
Diago. Seven days free — no card. Then five dollars a month. Every signal public. Even the losses.
```

Total narration: 97 words / 45s ≈ 2.1 wps.

## 5. Why this cut converts better

The rejected cut asked the viewer to trust Diago and only proved it at second 30; this one opens by paying the trust cost upfront — a published −94% loss in the first frame — which no competitor can copy without changing their business, so every claim that follows (2,259 nightly, 72,000 setups, the score) is heard as fact instead of marketing.
